Media

Choose each card’s shape and optional popup title below. The section fills your website’s width; its height is set separately. Local files are for preview; replace their URLs before exporting.

Using your media

Images: upload to Webflow Assets, then paste the asset link here.

Videos: use Vimeo or a direct hosted MP4/WebM link. Webflow’s regular Assets panel does not accept videos. Vimeo background playback requires a paid Vimeo plan and embedding permission.

For videos, add a poster image URL so the card also looks good before playback and with reduced motion.
